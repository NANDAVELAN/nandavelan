## 🚀 About Me

<div align="center">

<i>"Somewhere between a sensor reading and a dashboard, there's a system waiting to be built, and I like building all of it."</i>

<br/>

Hii ! I'm **Nandavelan S P S**, but most people call me **Nandy**. I'm a final-year **Electronics and Communication Engineering** student at **Saveetha Engineering College**, Chennai, pursuing a **Minor in Data Science**. I started out fascinated by how a tiny circuit can sense the world, and that curiosity slowly pulled me up the stack: from hardware and firmware, to backends and cloud, to AI models that make sense of the data.

Today I work as an intern at **Coovum (COO24 Technologies)**, an early-stage IoT and embedded startup, where I build full-scale applications with Python backends, Node.js frontends, and **Raspberry Pi and OpenCV** edge AI devices for quality inspection. Outside work, I love building things end to end: an **FPGA-based safety co-processor**, an **STM32 monitoring system**, and **RAG-powered learning tools**.

I'm currently diving deeper into **cloud and data engineering**, the **VLSI design flow**, and **agentic AI**, because I believe the best engineers can follow a signal from the sensor all the way to the screen. I learn by building, breaking things, and fixing them again.

*Open to internships, collaborations, and projects in edge AI, embedded systems, FPGA/VLSI, and cloud data engineering.*
